# GraveHopper

**Your cemetery adventures, organized.**

Ever been traveling and wondered whether you have relatives buried nearby? Or wanted to spend a weekend exploring cemeteries but didn't know where to start?

**GraveHopper** helps family-history researchers find relatives' burial places, plan cemetery visits, record what they discover, and keep track of information to add back to their genealogy database.

### [Open GraveHopper](https://jennfancher22-cpu.github.io/cemetery-fieldbook/) · [Quick Start](https://github.com/jennfancher22-cpu/cemetery-fieldbook/wiki/Quick-Start) · [Full Wiki](https://github.com/jennfancher22-cpu/cemetery-fieldbook/wiki)

## What you can do

- **Explore your family burials.** Import a GEDCOM (`.ged`) from your genealogy program and search for relatives, cemeteries, and places. See family relationships alongside burial records.
- **Plan cemetery outings.** Find cemeteries by area or distance and check which relatives are buried there.
- **Document fieldwork.** Record grave plots, visits, photo status, GPS information, notes, and new burial discoveries.
- **Keep photographs with discoveries.** Attach images from your computer or Android phone and view them in the burial record.
- **Work when offline.** Record fieldwork without a connection; when optional cloud synchronization is configured, changes can synchronize after connectivity returns.
- **Keep your genealogy database up to date.** Review a Change Log and printable PDF of information to enter into your family tree. Mark updates completed without losing activity history.
- **Refresh and check your data.** Compare a newer GEDCOM, run a read-only Data Integrity Audit, and use local or optional cloud backups.

**Important:** GraveHopper does **not** write changes directly into your genealogy software. You review and enter those updates yourself. Visit tracking fields such as *Visited*, *Photo Done*, and *GPS Recorded* are GraveHopper-specific; relevant fieldwork details can be appended to Burial Notes for transfer back to your tree.

## Get started

1. [Open GraveHopper](https://jennfancher22-cpu.github.io/cemetery-fieldbook/) in Chrome on your computer or Android device.
2. Export a GEDCOM (`.ged`) from your genealogy program.
3. Choose **Start with my family tree**, import the GEDCOM, and select your **Home Person**.
4. Optionally connect your personal **OneDrive** account to keep multiple devices synchronized. On a second device, choose **I already use GraveHopper on another device** instead of importing the GEDCOM again.
5. Select **Finish setup** and start exploring.

On Android, you can install the web app from Chrome's menu using **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**, depending on your device). No Play Store download is required.

For screenshots and step-by-step instructions, see the [Quick Start guide](https://github.com/jennfancher22-cpu/cemetery-fieldbook/wiki/Quick-Start).

## Data and privacy

- GraveHopper uses data from **your own GEDCOM import**. Your genealogy program remains the source of truth for your family tree.
- GraveHopper keeps its working data on your device. Cloud synchronization is **optional**.
- If you enable OneDrive synchronization or cloud backups, data is saved in your personal OneDrive app storage. **GraveHopper does not add its own encryption to those readable cloud data files.** Secure your OneDrive account accordingly.
- Synchronization is not a substitute for a separate backup of information you cannot recreate.
- **Do not commit personal GEDCOMs, exported backups, family photographs, or account credentials to this public repository.**

## Compatibility and project status

**Tested so far:** Family Tree Maker GEDCOM exports, Chrome on Windows, Android, and optional OneDrive synchronization between Windows and Android.

Other genealogy programs, browsers, iPhone/iPad, and other cloud providers have **not yet been verified**. The app is under active development, and behavior may change as testing continues.

Current tested branding release: **GraveHopper v7.23.9 LIVE** (November 2026). Check the version displayed in the app for the build you are running.
