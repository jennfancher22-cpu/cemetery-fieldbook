3 · Backup and restore Protect and transfer fieldwork

Backups and field notes

Upgrading? Keep your existing OneDrive backup and restore it in this version. Do not discard either version until you have verified the imported notes and photos.

Your edits and attached photos are kept on this device in this browser. Export a backup after each cemetery trip. The new full backup includes the original burial list, photos, notes, and change history. You can restore it here or on a different device.

Important: This is a single-file offline test, not a synced app. Browser storage may be erased, especially if you clear browsing data or open the file in a different app. Keep backups. Nearby search calculates straight-line miles for cemeteries with verified coordinates; it is not driving distance.
